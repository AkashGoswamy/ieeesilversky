# Limitations and claim boundaries

- Daily centroid positions omit within-day paths and GPS uncertainty; displacement is not raw GPS speed.
- The public release withholds waterpoint coordinates. Density peaks and clustered centroids are not verified waterpoints.
- Historical observations do not establish current conditions or causal ecological mechanisms.
- Missing collar-days and short environmental carry-forward imputation can influence apparent change; source missingness remains flagged.
- Robust thresholds and CUSUM parameters are analyst choices, not trained or externally validated clinical/ecological cutoffs.
- Synthetic events are controlled stress tests, not observed water scarcity, drought impact, or animal distress. Synthetic test performance cannot establish field accuracy or superiority.
- Groq outputs may be wrong or unavailable. The deterministic policy works without it; human review is required.
- Browser local storage is local to the browser profile and is not a managed audit database.
- No energy meter is included. Run-time is measured in-browser, but energy consumption is not inferred.
